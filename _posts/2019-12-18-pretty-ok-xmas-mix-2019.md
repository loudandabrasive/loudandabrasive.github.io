Far away from the blare of retail radios, nestled deep in crooks, crannies and kringles, there is indeed holiday music that is not good, per se, but is at the very least "Pretty Okay"

<!--more-->

Another year has gone by and I've assembled another hour of extremely tolerable holiday tunes for your enjoyment. I've resettled after last year's hike, in new ways and old. This year's album art offers a festive portrait (from an old "Galaxy" magazine) of an element of the spiritual essence of the newly Christened "Space Age Love Pad" that Gigs and I are calling home.

Best wishes to all, whether you're listening or not!
