Here as a respite from the droll, or even excruciating playlists from retail and radio is yet another playlist of "Pretty OK" Christmas music.

<!--more-->

This year takes the "eclectic" blend to a maximum, with quality lounge, international, rockabilly, soul, novelty, and even some acceptable classics, as well as oddities, such as the bookend sides from one of Sun Ra's Doo Wop groups. Sit back, relax, and try to forget how bad the rest of Christmas music can be.
