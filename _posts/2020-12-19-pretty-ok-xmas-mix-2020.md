Hi everyone,

This year's holiday season demands patience and hope, along with the joy. And while these feelings may seem unusual to the lucky among us, with a closer look, they aren't so foreign to yuletide.

<!--more-->

Every year boys and girls and children beyond the binary are anxiously awaiting something that seems impossible: the generous, magical, and always-pointing-at-something Santa Claus. So this year we pay tribute.

One thing that is foreign to yuletide, though, is good music. As in years past, with a closer look, I've found enough that is at least "Pretty OK" to fill an hour, including some gems I missed from last year. Watch out for some harsh language, though, since I've tried to capture some of our frustrations this year, too. So take a seat, pour yourself some cocoa (and maybe add a little something for daddy) and enjoy this year's "Pretty OK" Christmas mix.

Season's greetings,
